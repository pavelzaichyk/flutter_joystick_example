The App does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not request any device permissions.

The App is a demonstration of the open-source flutter_joystick package. All joystick input is processed locally on your device and is never sent anywhere.
